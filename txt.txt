A simple way to remember it

Think in terms of the number and type of variables:

1 numerical variable → Histogram, Box Plot, Violin Plot
1 categorical variable → Count Plot, Pie Chart (few categories)
2 numerical variables → Scatter Plot, Line Plot (if ordered by time)
1 numerical + 1 categorical → Box Plot, Violin Plot, Bar Plot
2 categorical variables → Count Plot with hue, Crosstab + Heatmap
Many numerical variables → Correlation Heatmap, Pairplot
